**Quan sát (trích vị trí)**
- **§5, PDF p5:** "Every 30 seconds, by default, the allocator receives the network's current routes and traffic… projects interface
  utilization… and generates a set of prefixes to shift from overloaded interfaces". Allocation "stateless", tạo lại từ đầu mỗi 30 s.
- **§5.1.2:** "calculates the average traffic rate for each prefix over a two-minute window. We use live rather than historical information
  because…".
- **§7.2:** "The median detour lasts 22 minutes, and 10% last at least 6 hours."
- **§7.3, PDF p10.** Theo §7, đây là kết quả "initial trial deployment at four PoPs".
  - Override chọn theo median latency đo, rồi "We left these overrides in place for 24 hours".
  - 45% prefix tốt hơn ít nhất 20 ms, nhưng "17% of prefixes experienced a median latency at least 20ms worse than the default path".
  - Tác giả "speculate": "(1) a path's performance is a function of the load placed onto it, and (2) a path's performance can change over
    time".
